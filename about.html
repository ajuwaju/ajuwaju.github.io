me

i'm allanah, from the philippines. i write about games, minds, and tech, and content writing is how i make a living. blog posts and seo articles, game guides and reviews, psychology and wellness pieces, beginner-friendly tech how-tos. i also edit and proofread. for a few years i wrote video scripts for other people's channels, which is where the scripts board comes from.

most evenings end in fortnite or valorant. i write about games the way you'd talk about them with a friend who plays.

what you get from me is research read at the source and an outline before the draft. the draft lands before the deadline and i reply within a few hours. i still get nervous before every first draft.

off the page i'm learning japanese. i'm sitting the jlpt n5 in december and saving for language school in tokyo, pencilled in for 2028. the japan journey posts here are my notes from the road.
